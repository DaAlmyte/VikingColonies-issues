# VikingColonies roadmap

What's coming to VikingColonies: the next update, the one or two after it, and ideas for later. This page follows the plan I work from and changes when the plan does.

_Last changed 5 November 2026._

## Now: 1.7.0

Planned for Sunday 8 November. Anything that isn't ready in time moves to the next update, so it doesn't hold up the rest.

- Huts that need you show a small sign you can see from across the colony
- The camp's window lists every settler who isn't working, and why: stuck, no tool, hungry, no bed or nothing to do
- The camp's and the huts' windows work with a controller
- A guard post you build where a Watchtower's guards should stand: a round shield on a pole with two crossed spears.
- Another player's camp can no longer take over your colony while its camp is down
- Settlers moving to a new camp leave ore and metal behind, as a portal would
- A camp taken down and put up again no longer brings four new settlers each time
- The forager can pick the bushes you planted yourself
- Your own settings (tips, small talk, language, window size) in the camp's window, no config file needed
- A language file, so players can translate the mod into their own language
- More workbench and cauldron upgrades in the huts, and a roomier Mead Hall
- Every colony window gets the same clear look as the camp's: boxes, tables and buttons where you need them
- Guards leave an animal you're taming alone unless it attacks someone
- The Mead Hall's kitchen: dishes you ask the cook to keep ready, and feasts.
- Settlers dance only round a pole that stands on its own, or a maypole, not round a house's corner post.
- Settlers left behind when you move your camp walk to the new one, and you can send a settler away.
- The Farm's field marker becomes a scarecrow.
- In creative mode, a Longhouse levelled up at once lets every settler walk to their bed
- A felled tree no longer lands on the end of a long wall or beam a few steps away
- Settlers swing their weapons and tools smoothly, without shaking
- Fixes for problems players reported: see the [known issues](guide/Known-Issues.md).

## Next

No dates from here on: two updates a week, new features on Sundays and fixes on Wednesdays.

### 1.7.1

- Settlers no longer talk about the stars or the sun on their face in the rain. (being built)
- A guard whose post they can't quite reach no longer steps back and forth at it. (being built)
- A settler standing in a doorway now steps aside when someone needs to get through. (being built)
- Two settlers meeting at a hut's door from its two sides no longer wait for each other all night. (being built)
- Hugin, the raven, no longer blocks a settler's way to a hut's door. (being built)
- Settlers running from a Troll no longer get caught at their hut's door. (being built)
- A new settler no longer appears inside a tree at the colony's edge, and one left behind no longer arrives in one. (being built)

### 1.8

- Settlers' own tools: each lumberjack, builder and farmer carries a real tool from your chests, which wears, gets repaired at your workbench or forge, and works faster the better it is (being built)
- Settlers change into their job's outfit when hired: a hat for every trade and Viking colours for every kind of work (being built)
- Quick start: one button on a new camp lays out its first huts on good spots, for you to accept or move (being built)
- Guards on watch look around, and turn to you when you come near. (being built)
- Settlers sometimes take a long way round a hut that stands by an edge of the game's walking map. We can now make it happen every time and are looking for the cause. (being built)
- Safer first days: early settlers are knocked out, not killed, until your first Longhouse stands (being built)
- Every hut's window gets the camp window's clear look: boxes, tables and lit tabs. (being built)
- Every hut gets a richer look at all five levels (being built)

## Later

Ideas I'd like to build. They may change, move or drop out.

- Our own memorial stone, with each fallen settler's name carved on it in runes.
- Put a hut's chest in a room of a house you built, and that room becomes the hut. Nothing of yours gets taken down.
- The mod in Spanish, Russian, German and Simplified Chinese
- One order raises every hut to the colony's new age after a boss
- Settlers' own gear, part 2: guards keep their own weapons and armour, workers wear armour from your chests, and pressing E on a settler shows and changes their gear
- Visitors at the camp: a wandering skald, a pedlar, a stranger asking to join
- The Deep North in the colony's progression: Kall's fall counted at the camp and celebrated, the Deep North's lands for trips, its tools, weapons and armour used by settlers and guards
- Choose which settler works each hut, see how fast each would work there and why, and every hut gets a clearer window that says why a worker is idle

## Just out: 1.6.1

- With a planting mod, the farmer pays a crop's full price in seeds
- With a planting mod, the lumberjack no longer plants Ashlands trees from beech seeds
- Do first can be taken off: on the order that is first, the button reads Not first
- Report a problem says what to do next in its own window, in fewer words
- A lumberjack's work speed (hunger, calling, nature and mood) counts on every axe blow
- A settler sits down on their place by the fire, not right up against it
- A hut you've just placed can't be broken by a passing animal before its builder starts
- On a colony's first day, settlers talk about different newcomers instead of all the same one.
- At breakfast settlers spread out round the chest instead of crowding into one spot (1.6.1).
- Sparring settlers stand closer to the training dummy (1.6.1).
- Building works with terrain mods such as Tunnelheim and Dig Deeper (1.6.1, Wednesday 4 November).
- Small fixes from earlier versions, checked and tidied.
- Hugin speaks one line at a time in a new colony, each when it makes sense
- A settler whose bed is taken away at night lies down again.
- The builder puts each piece up only after the pieces that hold it, so your own hut designs go up whole
- Settlers running from monsters spread over the colony's huts instead of crowding one door.
- Name tags stay over a sitting settler's head.

Everything in each update: the [changelog](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/DaAlmyte/VikingColonies/changelog/).
