# VikingColonies roadmap

What's coming to VikingColonies: the next update, the one or two after it, and ideas for later. This page follows the plan I work from and changes when the plan does.

_Last changed 12 November 2026._

## Now: 1.8.0

Planned for Sunday 15 November. Anything that isn't ready in time moves to the next update, so it doesn't hold up the rest.

- Settlers' own tools: each lumberjack, builder and farmer carries a real tool from your chests, which wears, gets repaired at your workbench or forge, and works faster the better it is
- Settlers change into their job's outfit when hired: a hat for every trade and Viking colours for every kind of work
- Quick start: one button on a new camp lays out its first huts on good spots, for you to accept or move
- Guards get more health from the armour they wear, so a guard in iron can stand up to a Troll
- Settlers pick better places to stand: out of a fire's reach, and not in a doorway, in a rock or on a bench.
- Guards walk real rounds of the colony, past several huts, before they go back to their post
- See where your colony's border runs: on the map, on the ground, and how far a Watchtower pushes it out
- Farmers hold their own cultivator and swing it when they work new farmland and plant.
- Guards on watch look around, and turn to you when you come near.
- Each settler goes to the fire, to bed and out in the morning at their own moment.
- Hang a sign by a hut's door and leave it empty: it gets the hut's mark and name. Your own words on a sign stay.
- Safer first days: early settlers are knocked out, not killed, until your first Longhouse stands
- Every hut's window gets the camp window's clear look: boxes, tables and lit tabs.
- Every hut gets a richer look at all five levels
- A felled tree no longer lands on the end of a long wall or beam a few steps away
- The cook takes free time like everyone else when the meals are done.
- Settlers earn bynames from what they do, like Ulf Tree-feller or Bjorn Troll-bane
- Settlers answer each other when they chat.
- Fixes for problems players reported: see the [known issues](guide/Known-Issues.md).

## Next

No dates from here on: two updates a week, new features on Sundays and fixes on Wednesdays.

### 1.9

- Our own memorial stone, with each fallen settler's name carved on it in runes. (being built)
- A settler who stops right outside a door now shuts it behind them.
- In creative mode, a Longhouse levelled up at once lets every settler walk to their bed
- Settlers swing their weapons and tools smoothly, without shaking

### 1.10

- The mod in Spanish, Russian, German and Simplified Chinese
- One order raises every hut to the colony's new age after a boss
- Guard posts can stand anywhere in your colony, and the guards keep watch around the post.

## Later

Ideas I'd like to build. They may change, move or drop out.

- The Deep North in the colony's progression: Kall's fall counted at the camp and celebrated, the Deep North's lands for trips, its tools, weapons and armour used by settlers and guards
- When you come home after a day or more, Hugin tells you what happened while you were away, what needs you first; the mine catches up on the days nobody was near
- Your settlers' trips under way pinned on your own map, and a Far work list in the camp window
- Put a hut's chest in a room of a house you built, and that room becomes the hut. Nothing of yours gets taken down.
- Send settlers on trips to biomes you've visited: they come back with what doesn't grow at home
- Settlers' own gear, part 2: guards keep their own weapons and armour, workers wear armour from your chests, and pressing E on a settler shows and changes their gear
- Visitors at the camp: a wandering skald, a pedlar, a stranger asking to join
- Choose which settler works each hut, see how fast each would work there and why, and every hut gets a clearer window that says why a worker is idle

## Just out: 1.7.1

- A quick double click with the hammer no longer raises two memorial stones in one colony.
- A lumberjack no longer stands waiting again and again by a log that won't stop rocking.
- Settlers who sat down from on top of a bench get up onto the floor, not back onto the bench.
- The Mead Hall's cook finds a place to stand inside the hall at every station, so a station by a wall or the door no longer leaves them stuck.
- A settler's last swing when a fight ends no longer lands on an animal or a wall that happens to be in its way. Fixed in 1.7.1.
- Settlers no longer talk about the stars or the sun on their face in the rain.
- A guard whose post they can't quite reach no longer steps back and forth at it.
- A settler standing in a doorway now steps aside when someone needs to get through.
- Two settlers meeting at a hut's door from its two sides no longer wait for each other all night.
- Hugin, the raven, no longer blocks a settler's way to a hut's door.
- Settlers running from a Troll no longer get caught at their hut's door.
- A new settler no longer appears inside a tree at the colony's edge, and one left behind no longer arrives in one.

Everything in each update: the [changelog](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/DaAlmyte/VikingColonies/changelog/).
