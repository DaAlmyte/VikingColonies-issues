# VikingColonies roadmap

What's coming to VikingColonies: the next update, the one or two after it, and ideas for later. It's written from the plan we work from, and changes by itself when the plan does.

_Last changed 29 October 2026._

## Now: 1.6.0

Planned for Sunday 1 November. If something isn't ready in time, it moves to the next update instead of holding the rest back.

- A cook in a Mead Hall: roasting that never burns and hot breakfasts for the settlers, in a new hall with five levels
- Placing a hut says which way and how far it will grow, so you leave room for its upgrades.
- The memorial stone looks like one of Valheim's runestones.
- The Chieftain's Camp's Overview gets its proper look: boxes, big numbers and colours.
- Saving a hut design under a name you used before keeps a backup of the old one
- Your own pieces in a hut (a bench, a lantern, a banner) stay put when the hut is upgraded
- Report a problem also offers the Discord, and says where your bug report file is saved
- Pastimes: settlers spend their free time at archery, sparring, board games, stories by the fire and more
- The builder puts up their own hut first, and says why when they move to another site
- Fixes for problems players reported: see the [known issues](guide/Known-Issues.md).

## Next

No dates from here on: two updates a week, new features on Sundays and fixes on Wednesdays.

### 1.6.1

- On a colony's first day, settlers talk about different newcomers instead of all the same one. (being built)
- At breakfast settlers spread out round the chest instead of crowding into one spot (1.6.1). (being built)
- Sparring settlers stand closer to the training dummy (1.6.1). (being built)
- Building works with terrain mods such as Tunnelheim and Dig Deeper (1.6.1, Wednesday 4 November). (being built)
- Hugin speaks one line at a time in a new colony, each when it makes sense (being built)
- Settlers running from monsters spread over the colony's huts instead of crowding one door. (being built)
- Hugin, the raven, no longer blocks a settler's way to a hut's door.
- Small fixes from earlier versions, checked and tidied.
- A settler whose bed is taken away at night lies down again.
- The builder puts each piece up only after the pieces that hold it, so your own hut designs go up whole
- Settlers running from a Troll no longer get caught at their hut's door.
- Name tags stay over a sitting settler's head.

### 1.7

- Guards leave an animal you're taming alone unless it attacks someone (being built)
- Settlers dance only round a pole that stands on its own, or a maypole, not round a house's corner post. (being built)
- Settlers left behind when you move your camp walk to the new one, and you can send a settler away. (being built)
- The Farm's field marker becomes a scarecrow. (being built)

## Later

Ideas we'd like to build. They may change, move or drop out.

- Every hut's window gets the camp window's clear look: boxes, tables and lit tabs.
- Every colony window gets the same clear look as the camp's: boxes, tables and buttons where you need them
- Our own memorial stone, with each fallen settler's name carved on it in runes.
- The Mead Hall's kitchen: dishes you ask the cook to keep ready, and feasts.
- Every hut gets a richer look at all five levels
- Settlers' own tools: each lumberjack, builder and farmer carries a real tool from your chests, which wears, gets repaired at your workbench or forge, and works faster the better it is
- Settlers change into their job's outfit when hired: a hat for every trade and Viking colours for every kind of work
- The mod in Spanish, Russian, German and Simplified Chinese

## Just out: 1.5.1

- The Town Hall becomes the Chieftain's Camp, with a new window: an overview, a page for each settler, the colony's saga and a size setting
- A memorial stone that names the settlers your colony has lost, and a setting that lets the fallen walk back in after a few days
- Hugin the raven guides you through your first colony, one step at a time
- Settlers break off a fight the moment they're hurt to half, and brave settlers run from Trolls in time
- Settlers head straight for a hut's door instead of walking round the back first
- A settler sheltering from a monster stays inside instead of walking out past it
- Materials you put in a hut's chest stay there for that hut's own upgrade

Everything in each update: the [changelog](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/DaAlmyte/VikingColonies/changelog/).

## Your ideas

Post them in #feedback on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/yCzDUPQA3D), one post per idea with the Idea tag, or on the [issues page](https://github.com/DaAlmyte/VikingColonies-issues/issues/new/choose). Every post is read, and the 👍 count on posts helps decide what comes next.
