# Getting started

1. Install with a mod manager: **Jotunn**, then **VikingColonies**. Start the game through the mod manager.
2. Build a **workbench**. The Chieftain's Camp is in the hammer's **Misc** tab and only shows near a workbench.
3. Build a **Chieftain's Camp** (20 Wood, 10 Stone; called the Town Hall before 1.5.1). Four settlers hear of it and walk in from the edge of the colony, a
   builder among them. Its chest (**E**) is the colony's store.
4. Build a **Builder's Hut** (20 Wood) first. Like every hut it is in the hammer's **Misc** tab and shows while you
   stand inside the colony's area (rings show it while you place a hut). A citizen becomes the builder
   straight away and puts up their own hut's building first, piece by piece.
5. Give the builder what they need, in the camp's chest or their hut's chest:
   - **Wood** for the buildings (about 35 for their own hut, 60 for a Lumberjack's Hut) and some **Stone** for
     flattening the ground.
   - A **flint axe**, an **antler pickaxe** and a **hoe** to clear and flatten sites. They work with copies; your tools
     stay in the chest.

   When they are missing something, hovering over them and their hut's window (**Shift+E**) say "Needs …".
6. Build a **Lumberjack's Hut** (20 Wood) near trees. Its outline shows **green** (ready), **yellow** (the builder
   clears the site first; the line under the crosshair says what) or **red** (it can't go there, and says why). Rings
   show the colony area and the hut's 40 m work area.
7. Once the builder has finished it, a citizen is hired as the lumberjack and starts cutting trees. Wood ends up in the
   hut's storage (**E**). Put a **Cultivator** in it and they replant too; its **Trees…** list chooses which kinds they
   fell and replant, and they leave trees by your buildings standing.
8. Build a **Longhouse** (20 Wood): its beds are your settlers' homes (2 at level 1, 2 more per level). At night
   settlers go indoors to sleep. A free bed and food in the camp's chest bring a **newcomer** once a day.
9. Keep **food** in the colony's chests (the camp keeps 10 of it for newcomers: its **Keep in stock** tab, which
   every hut has too, lists what a chest keeps; settlers with free time carry what's missing): every settler eats once a day, the plainest food first, and a hungry one works
   at half speed. A **Forager's Hut** (20 Wood) brings home wild berries and mushrooms (and herbs such as dandelions
   for you); its **What to pick…** list chooses the kinds (tick flint and branches there if you want them too). Later, a **Farm** (20 Wood) with a Cultivator and seeds in its chest
   grows crops on the fields you mark: put a **field marker** (Misc tab) near it, press **E** on it to choose a crop and
   a size (one field per Farm level): the colony's steady food.
10. **Shift+E** on a hut manages its worker, its level and upgrades. **Shift+E** on the camp opens on its
    **Overview** (the colony at a glance), then **People** (everyone, with a page each and Recall), **Work**
    (everything the builders have to do, what it still needs, and Do first / Set aside), the **Saga** (what happened
    in the colony, and its deeds) and **The camp** (its levels and its Keep in stock).
11. **Grow the colony with your journey:** after the Forsaken, hang their trophies on item stands by the camp (or
    put them in its chest) and raise the camp a level in **The camp** tab: a wider colony, room for more settlers and
    the next age of huts (huts go as high as the camp). Eikthyr raises it to level 2, The Elder and Bonemass to 3,
    Moder and Yagluth to 4, the Queen to 5. The trophies aren't used up, and a boss your world has already beaten
    counts without its trophy.
12. **Stop hauling (1.5):** when goods pile up in the huts, build a **Storehouse** (20 Wood, once the colony has a
    Builder's Hut; one per colony) and hire a courier in its window. Couriers fill what each building keeps in stock and
    what the builder needs, and take the huts' surplus to the storehouse. After a trip, empty your bag into the
    storehouse's own chest (the one with its sign): it's put away like with like. Its **Needs** tab says what the
    colony is short of, and what to bring. See [Buildings](Buildings.md#storehouse).

**Just building?** In a world with the **No build cost** setting, creative mode places colony buildings already built
and adds Build now, Upgrade now and Add settlers to their windows: see [Creative mode](Buildings.md#creative-mode-15) (1.5).

**Give them somewhere to sit.** The builder puts up the camp (1.4) after the huts: a fire pit by its chest,
with seats as the camp rises. A few benches and a campfire near each hut help too: settlers with nothing to do sit
there, warm themselves, chat and greet you. A table with benches by the camp is where they eat breakfast, and in
the evening they gather round the camp's fire (or the fire nearest the camp; put some wood in the camp's
chest: they keep it burning).

**Keep them content.** Each settler has a mood: a bed of their own (a cosy Longhouse with a fire, rugs and a table is
better), a meal every day and not the same one each time, and an evening by a burning fire make them content or joyful,
and they work faster. Hungry, homeless or grieving they're unhappy and slower, and no newcomer joins an unhappy colony.
Hover over a settler to see their mood; choose them in the camp's People tab to see why.

Once the huts stand, the builder keeps them in repair: with nothing to build they mend damaged pieces, and put back
pieces a troll or a fire broke (never what you removed with your hammer).

**Help as you go:** Hugin, the raven, explains each step the first time it comes up (it needs the game's raven hints on:
Settings, "Enable raven hints"; the mod's `Messages.HuginTips` turns Hugin's colony tips off). Since 1.5.1 Hugin also says what to do next, seven steps from a Builder's Hut to
the camp's level 2, and the camp's Overview shows the next one. A settler near you who needs something says so above their head ("Needs 12 Wood",
"Storage full", "Hungry").
