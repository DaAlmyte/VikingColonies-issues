# Getting started

1. Install with a mod manager: **Jotunn**, then **VikingColonies**. Start the game through the mod manager.
2. Build a **workbench**. The Town Hall is in the hammer's **Misc** tab and only shows near a workbench.
3. Build a **Town Hall** (20 Wood, 10 Stone). Four settlers hear of it and walk in from the edge of the colony, a
   builder among them. Its chest (**E**) is the colony's store.
4. Build a **Builder's Hut** (20 Wood) first. Like every hut it is in the hammer's **Misc** tab and shows while you
   stand inside the Town Hall's colony area (rings show it while you place a hut). A citizen becomes the builder
   straight away and puts up his own hut's building first, piece by piece.
5. Give the builder what he needs, in the Town Hall's chest or his hut's chest:
   - **Wood** for the buildings (about 35 for his own hut, 60 for a Lumberjack's Hut) and some **Stone** for
     flattening the ground.
   - A **flint axe**, an **antler pickaxe** and a **hoe** to clear and flatten sites. He works with copies; your tools
     stay in the chest.

   When he is missing something, hovering over him and his hut's window (**Shift+E**) say "Needs …".
6. Build a **Lumberjack's Hut** (20 Wood) near trees. Its outline shows **green** (ready), **yellow** (the builder
   clears the site first; the line under the crosshair says what) or **red** (it can't go there, and says why). Rings
   show the colony area and the hut's 40 m work area.
7. Once the builder has finished it, a citizen is hired as the lumberjack and starts cutting trees. Wood ends up in the
   hut's storage (**E**). Put a **Cultivator** in it and he replants too; its **Trees…** list chooses which kinds he
   fells and replants, and he leaves trees by your buildings standing.
8. Build a **Longhouse** (20 Wood): its beds are your settlers' homes (2 at level 1, 2 more per level). At night
   settlers go indoors to sleep. A free bed and food in the Town Hall's chest bring a **newcomer** once a day.
9. Keep **food** in the colony's chests (the Town Hall keeps 10 of it for newcomers: its **Keep in stock** tab, which
   every hut has too, lists what a chest keeps; settlers with free time carry what's missing): every settler eats once a day, the plainest food first, and a hungry one works
   at half speed. A **Forager's Hut** (20 Wood) brings home wild berries and mushrooms (and herbs such as dandelions
   for you); its **What to pick…** list chooses the kinds (tick flint and branches there if you want them too). Later, a **Farm** (20 Wood) with a Cultivator and seeds in its chest
   grows crops on the fields you mark: put a **field marker** (Misc tab) near it, press **E** on it to choose a crop and
   a size (one field per Farm level): the colony's steady food.
10. **Shift+E** on a hut manages its worker, its level and upgrades. **Shift+E** on the Town Hall has three tabs:
    **Citizens** (everyone, with Recall), the **Saga** (what happened in the colony) and **Work orders** (everything
    the builders have to do, what it still needs, and Do first / Set aside).

**Give them somewhere to sit.** A few benches and a campfire near the Town Hall and each hut: settlers with nothing
to do sit there, warm themselves, chat and greet you. A table with benches by the Town Hall is where they eat
breakfast, and in the evening they gather round the fire nearest the Town Hall (put some wood in the Town Hall's chest:
they keep it burning).

**Keep them content.** Each settler has a mood: a bed of his own (a cosy Longhouse with a fire, rugs and a table is
better), a meal every day and not the same one each time, and an evening by a burning fire make him content or joyful,
and he works faster. Hungry, homeless or grieving he's unhappy and slower, and no newcomer joins an unhappy colony.
Hover over a settler to see his mood; choose him in the Town Hall's Citizens tab to see why.

Once the huts stand, the builder keeps them in repair: with nothing to build he mends damaged pieces, and puts back
pieces a troll or a fire broke (never what you removed with your hammer).

**Help as you go:** Hugin, the raven, explains each step the first time it comes up (turn the game's tutorials on, or
the mod's `Messages.HuginTips` off). A settler near you who needs something says so above his head ("Needs 12 Wood",
"Storage full", "Hungry").
