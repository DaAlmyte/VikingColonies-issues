# Getting started

1. Install with a mod manager: **Jotunn**, then **VikingColonies**. Start the game through the mod manager.
2. Build a **workbench**. The Chieftain's Camp is in the hammer's **Misc** tab and only shows near a workbench.
3. Build a **Chieftain's Camp** (20 Wood, 10 Stone; called the Town Hall before 1.5.1). Four settlers hear of it and
   walk in from the edge of the colony, and one of them is a builder. The camp's chest (**E**) is the colony's store.
   A new camp brings settlers only until you have that many in all (four, unless the server changed it): a second camp, or one put up again after you moved,
   grows by newcomers and by the settlers who walk over from your old camp. Your colony belongs to the character who
   founded it; a new character starts a new one.
4. Build a **Builder's Hut** (20 Wood) first. Like every hut it is in the hammer's **Misc** tab, and it shows while you
   stand inside the colony's area (rings mark that area while you place a hut). A citizen becomes the builder
   straight away and puts up their own hut's building first, piece by piece.
5. Give the builder what they need, in the camp's chest or their hut's chest:
   - Wood for the buildings (about 35 for their own hut, 60 for a Lumberjack's Hut), plus some Stone for flattening
     the ground.
   - A flint axe, an antler pickaxe and a hoe to clear and flatten sites. The builder works with copies, so your tools
     stay in the chest.

   When the builder is missing something, hovering over them or opening their hut's window (**Shift+E**) says
   "Needs …".
6. Build a **Lumberjack's Hut** (20 Wood) near trees. Its outline shows green when the hut is ready to go there,
   yellow when the builder has to clear the site first (the line under the crosshair says what), and red when it
   can't go there (the same line says why). Rings show the colony area and the hut's 40 m work area.
7. When the builder has finished it, a citizen is hired as the lumberjack and starts cutting trees. The wood ends up in
   the hut's storage (**E**). Put a **Cultivator** in the storage and the lumberjack replants too. The hut's
   **Trees…** list chooses which kinds they fell and replant, and they leave trees by your buildings standing.
8. Build a **Longhouse** (20 Wood). Its beds are your settlers' homes, 2 at level 1 and 2 more per level, and at night
   settlers go indoors to sleep. A free bed and food in the camp's chest bring a newcomer once a day.
9. Keep food in the colony's chests. Every settler eats once a day, the plainest food first, and a hungry one works
   at half speed. The camp keeps 10 of it for newcomers. Its **Keep in stock** tab, which every hut has too, lists what
   a chest keeps, and settlers with free time carry what's missing. A **Forager's Hut** (20 Wood) brings home wild
   berries and mushrooms (and herbs such as dandelions for you), and its **What to pick…** list chooses the kinds
   (tick flint and branches there if you want them too). Later, a **Farm** (20 Wood) with a Cultivator and seeds in its
   chest grows crops on the fields you mark. Put a **field marker** (Misc tab) near it, then press **E** on the marker
   to choose a crop and a size (one field per Farm level). That is the colony's steady food.
10. Press **Shift+E** on a hut to manage its worker, its level and upgrades (with a controller, the game's alternate
    use button and Use together, as its hover line says). On the camp it opens on the **Overview** (the colony at a glance) and has more tabs: **People** (everyone, with a page each and Recall), **Work**
    (everything the builders have to do, what it still needs, and Do first / Set aside), the **Saga** (what happened
    in the colony, and its deeds) and **The camp** (its levels and its Keep in stock).
11. The colony grows with your journey. Once you have beaten the Forsaken, hang their trophies on item stands by the
    camp (or put them in its chest) and raise the camp a level in **The camp** tab. That gives a wider colony, room for
    more settlers and the next age of huts (huts go as high as the camp). Eikthyr raises it to level 2, The Elder and
    Bonemass to 3, Moder and Yagluth to 4, the Queen to 5. The trophies aren't used up, and a boss your world has
    already beaten counts without its trophy.
12. To stop hauling (1.5), build a **Storehouse** (20 Wood, once the colony has a Builder's Hut; one per colony) when
    goods pile up in the huts, and hire a courier in its window. Couriers fill what each building keeps in stock and
    what the builder needs, and take the huts' surplus to the storehouse. After a trip, empty your bag into the
    storehouse's own chest (the one with its sign), and it's put away like with like. Its **Needs** tab says what the
    colony is short of and what to bring. See [Buildings](Buildings.md#storehouse).

If you just want to build, a world with the **No build cost** setting lets creative mode place colony buildings
already built, and adds Build now, Upgrade now and Add settlers to their windows. See
[Creative mode](Buildings.md#creative-mode-15) (1.5).

The builder puts up the camp (1.4) after the huts, with a fire pit by its chest and seats as the camp rises. A few
benches and a campfire near each hut help too. Settlers with nothing to do sit there, warm themselves, chat and greet
you. They eat breakfast at a table with benches by the camp, and in the evening they gather round the camp's fire, or
the fire nearest the camp. Put some wood in the camp's chest and they keep it burning.

Each settler has a mood. A bed of their own (a cosy Longhouse with a fire, rugs and a table is better), a meal every
day and not the same one each time, and an evening by a burning fire make them content or joyful, and they work faster.
Hungry, homeless or grieving, they're unhappy and slower, and no newcomer joins an unhappy colony. Hover over a settler
to see their mood, or choose them in the camp's People tab to see why.

Once the huts stand, the builder keeps them in repair. With nothing to build, they mend damaged pieces and put back
pieces a troll or a fire broke (never what you removed with your hammer).

Hugin, the raven, explains each step the first time it comes up. This needs the game's raven hints on (Settings,
"Enable raven hints"), and the mod's `Messages.HuginTips` turns Hugin's colony tips off. Since 1.5.1 Hugin also says
what to do next, in seven steps from a Builder's Hut to the camp's level 2, and the camp's Overview shows the next one.
A settler near you who needs something says so above their head ("Needs 12 Wood", "Storage full", "Hungry").
