# Known issues

Problems in VikingColonies we know about, the worst first: what's being done, and a way round them where there is one.

_Last updated 19 October 2026._

VikingColonies 1.4.0 · Works with the current Valheim (checked 19 October 2026).

Is your problem not here? [Report it](https://github.com/DaAlmyte/VikingColonies-issues/issues/new/choose), or press
**Report a problem** in the Town Hall's window. Look at [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md) first: it explains the
things that work that way on purpose.

- **The camp or a hut goes up into the side of a big boulder.** The builder breaks up the rocks standing on a
  building's lot, but not yet a big boulder whose middle lies beside it and only its side reaches in. **Way round:**
  leave a few metres between the Town Hall (or a hut) and big boulders, or break the rock back yourself with a pickaxe.
  Being fixed for 1.4.1.
- **Settlers hide from Necks again and again near water.** Settlers run from monsters that hunt them, and a Neck by a
  pond keeps coming back, so the colony stops to hide over and over. **Way round:** put the colony a little away from
  ponds, build a Watchtower (its guards deal with monsters in the colony), or clear the Necks yourself. Settlers that
  stand up to weak animals are planned.
- **Settlers' speech can show at odd places on the screen** when they are just out of view. Being looked at.
- **Settlers bump into each other and into you** and push one another aside as they walk. Being looked at.
